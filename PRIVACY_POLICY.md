# Privacy Policy — Telegram Proxy Opener

**Last updated:** July 27, 2026

Telegram Proxy Opener ("the App") is a simple utility that helps you find and connect to public MTProto and SOCKS5 proxy servers for Telegram. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is used.

## Data We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.** The App has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting services that send data off your device.

## Network Activity

To do its job, the App makes the following network requests directly from your device:

1. **Fetching proxy lists** — the App downloads publicly available lists of MTProto and SOCKS5 proxy servers from third-party GitHub-hosted repositories (community-maintained lists of public Telegram proxies). No information about you or your device is sent as part of these requests beyond what any standard HTTP request includes (e.g., your IP address, which is visible to any server you connect to).
2. **Checking proxy availability** — the App attempts a short TCP connection to each candidate proxy server to check whether it is currently reachable, before showing it to you as an option.
3. **Opening Telegram** — when you choose a proxy, the App hands off to the Telegram app (or the Play Store, if Telegram isn't installed) via a standard Android deep link. The App does not intercept, log, or store your Telegram traffic — once you connect, your traffic goes directly between your device, the proxy server, and Telegram.

## Third-Party Proxy Servers

The proxy servers listed are operated by unrelated third parties, not by us. We do not control these servers and cannot vouch for their operators. Traffic routed through a public proxy is visible to that proxy's operator. Use public proxies at your own discretion.

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any user data to third parties, because we do not collect any in the first place.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
