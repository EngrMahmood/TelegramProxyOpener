Telegram Proxy Opener helps you quickly find and connect to publicly available
MTProto and SOCKS5 proxy servers for Telegram — useful when Telegram is slow,
restricted, or blocked on your network.

HOW IT WORKS
• The app fetches a list of public proxy servers from community-maintained sources
• Each proxy is checked for live availability before you pick one
• Tap "Open in Telegram" and the app hands off directly to Telegram using its
  built-in proxy connection feature — no configuration, no typing in server
  addresses by hand
• If Telegram isn't installed, you'll be sent to its Play Store page instead

WHAT THIS APP DOES NOT DO
• It does not operate, host, or control any of the listed proxy servers —
  they're run by independent third parties
• It does not intercept, log, or store your Telegram traffic
• It does not require an account or collect personal information

Because proxy servers are run by third parties outside our control, we can't
guarantee uptime, speed, or the trustworthiness of any individual server.
Traffic routed through a public proxy is visible to that proxy's operator, so
use public proxies at your own discretion.
